Who We Are

Leading the Sustainable Agri-Food Value Chain in Africa

Our commitment to uncompromised quality, strict international standards, and ethical farm partnerships positions us as a trusted supplier for global food manufacturers, animal feed producers, and industrial processors.

ESG & Responsibility

Nurturing the Earth for Future Generations

At HADELMA NIG LTD, sustainability is not an afterthought — it is at the heart of our commercial strategy. We balance agricultural productivity with environmental conservation.

  • Responsible Agriculture: Minimizing chemical inputs through bio-friendly pest control and crop rotation.
  • Soil Conservation: Implementing zero-tillage techniques and cover cropping to maintain soil organic matter.
  • Water Management: Utilizing smart drip irrigation to reduce water waste in dry seasons.
  • Renewable Energy: Transitioning our processing facilities toward solar-powered auxiliary systems.
  • Waste Reduction: Upcycling agro-industrial organic waste into biofuel biomass and bio-fertilizer.

Frequently Asked Questions

For international shipments, our standard minimum order quantity starts at 1 Full Container Load (20ft or 40ft FCL depending on the commodity). For domestic supply in Nigeria, we accommodate custom truckload sizes.

All products undergo strict laboratory testing for moisture content, purity, and aflatoxin levels before shipment. We provide official Phytosanitary Certificates issued by the Nigerian Agricultural Quarantine Service (NAQS) alongside SGS or Intertek inspection reports upon request.

Our operational headquarters and processing hubs are located at Water Tank Street, Aliade, Gwer Local Government Area (LGA), Benue State, Nigeria. We also maintain logistics operations near major export sea ports in Lagos and Port Harcourt.

We work with internationally recognized payment instruments including Irrevocable & Confirmed Letters of Credit (LC at Sight) and Telegraphic Transfers (T/T) according to agreed contractual milestones.
